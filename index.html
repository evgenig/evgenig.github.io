Main Features

🧭 Smart Navigation

Navigate to saved waypoints using a guiding arrow, voice navigation, and real-time distance tracking.

📍 Save Locations

Quickly save your parked car, campsite, shelter, hotel, fishing spot, or any important place.

🛰 Offline Support

Works offline and even on devices without a compass sensor.

🗺 Route Tracking

Record tracks, follow saved routes, or backtrack your path anytime.

🌞 Outdoor Tools

Includes sunrise/sunset times, sun & moon positions, and HUD mode.

📂 GPX Import/Export

Import and export GPX files to share tracks and waypoints easily.

Advanced Navigation Information

  • Current address and GPS coordinates
  • Distance to destination
  • Current speed and ETA
  • GPS accuracy and altitude
  • Magnetic field strength
  • Trip computer statistics
  • Travel path visualization
  • Support for Decimal, DMS, MGRS and what3words coordinates

Designed for Outdoor Adventures

  • Simple and user-friendly interface
  • Metric and imperial units support
  • Power-saving mode
  • Magnetic declination correction
  • Special car mode for reliable vehicle navigation
  • Navigation available on lock screen
Important:

Devices without a compass (magnetometer) sensor can still use the app, but the navigation arrow updates only while moving. For best accuracy, keep the top of your device pointed in the direction of travel.
