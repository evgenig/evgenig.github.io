What you can monitor

Environment

Magnetic field and individual axes, ambient light, proximity, atmospheric pressure, ambient and battery temperature, and humidity.

Motion & vibration

Acceleration, linear vibration, gravity, tilt, rotation, and vibration RMS, peak, and dominant frequency.

Sound

Microphone level, dominant frequency, a live spectrum, and sudden sound impulses.

Location & signals

GPS speed, accuracy, satellites, fix age, location jumps, GNSS signal quality, a location perimeter, Wi-Fi, and cellular signal.

Experimental

Camera brightness and scene motion, near-ultrasound presence detection, and on-screen touch activity.

Good to know

  • Available cards depend on your device's hardware, Android version, and the permissions you grant.
  • Readings are not calibrated professional measurements and are not intended for safety-critical use.
  • GPS jumps, weak satellite signals, camera motion, and sound impulses can have many causes. They do not prove jamming, spoofing, or interference.
  • Recordings stay on your device. Camera frames are analyzed locally, and no images or video are saved.
  • The ultrasound feature emits a short 18.5 kHz tone, which may be audible on some devices.
